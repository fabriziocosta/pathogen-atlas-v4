# Rice-virus Explorer profiles

The Explorer has a rice-specific literature layer for 20 viruses reported to infect rice. It records plant symptoms and transmission vectors from [Wang et al. 2022, PMID 36298813](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36298813/), with more recent PubMed pair screening for taxa in the pinned ICTV catalog. Traits not described in the source are blank; they are not treated as biological absences.

The 17 records resolved to current ICTV taxa are RDV, RSV, RBSDV, RGSV, RTBV, RTSV, RYSV, RRSV, RGDV, RNMV, SRBSDV, RSMV, RDaBV, RYMV, RSNV, RHBV, and RDPV. Three names in the review are not mapped to a current ICTV species in the pinned taxonomy: rice bunchy stunt virus (RBSV), rice virus A (RVA), and rice curl dwarf-associated virus (RCDaV). They appear as literature-associated records with stable local IDs and are not represented as accepted ICTV taxa. RVA and RCDaV use their discovery papers ([RVA, PMID 28695276](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28695276/); [RCDaV, PMID 34721366](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34721366/)) for the species-specific annotation. RDPV's symptom and vector traits come from its primary study ([PMID 41649689](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41649689/)).

Rice trait dimensions cover plant stunting/dwarfing, tiller changes, leaf yellowing/chlorosis, leaf striping/mosaic, leaf distortion/galls, and transmission by planthoppers, leafhoppers, whiteflies/aphids, soil-borne fungi, or beetles. Rice-virus coordinates are fitted with IMSTE from the 20-profile, 12-feature literature presence matrix after standardization. The small cohort uses 10 ranked spanning trees; the V2 atlas setting of 64 trees collapses at this sample size. Zero is used only as a computational not-recorded marker for feature-space distance; the Explorer keeps the biological feature blank where the cited source did not report it. Coordinates and fit metadata are recorded in the Explorer payload under `embedding_runs[ncbi:4530]`.

The rice refresh searches the 17 ICTV-mapped taxa with current ICTV names plus unique ICTV Virus name(s) and Virus name abbreviation(s), and screens up to 20 sampled abstracts per pair using Jev `typesafe/jev-1.14` at the configured 0.7 positive threshold. Fourteen of those 17 mapped taxa have at least one accepted sampled PMID in this refresh. The literature-listed RBSV name and three tentative names were also queried separately; PubMed returned no matching records for RBSV, while the RVA and RCDaV samples did not pass the Jev pair screen. The compact output in `sites/pathogen-atlas-explorer/source/rice_virus_screen.json` records pair counts and positive PMID evidence. The human profile source remains `sites/pathogen-atlas-explorer/source/human_pair_data.json.gz` and is not rewritten by the rice layer.
